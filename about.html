Our Story

Empower. Educate. Uplift.

Founded in 2015, Human Team Foundation (HTF) is a Mumbai-based charitable trust working to uplift disadvantaged communities through targeted, lasting initiatives.

We dedicate ourselves to empowering underprivileged communities through education and skill development — aiming to inspire self-sufficiency and resilience. Over the years we have empowered thousands to achieve independence and foster community growth.

From classrooms to skill workshops to mental-health support, everything we do is rooted in a simple idea: that we rise by lifting others.

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged communities through education and skill development — building self-sufficiency, dignity and resilience, and helping every individual reach their full potential.

Our Vision

A society where every person — regardless of background — has access to quality education, meaningful skills and the opportunity to rise and live with dignity.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Three principles shape every decision we make and every life we touch.

Integrity

We build trust with the communities we serve through honesty, accountability and full transparency.

Compassion

We lead with empathy, meeting every person with kindness, respect and genuine care.

Collaboration

We work hand-in-hand with communities, partners and volunteers to maximise our collective impact.

Our Approach

Real skills. Lasting change.

We don't just offer aid — we build ability. Through tutoring, mentorship, vocational workshops and wellbeing support, we equip people with what they need to stand on their own.

Every program is designed around the real needs of the community, delivered with care, and measured by the lives it changes.
