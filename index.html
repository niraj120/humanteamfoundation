Who We Are

Empower. Educate. Uplift.

Founded in 2015, Human Team Foundation is a Mumbai-based charitable trust dedicated to empowering underprivileged communities through education and skill development — inspiring self-sufficiency, dignity and resilience.

  • Free education & mentorship for children and youth
  • Skill development that builds real employability
  • Guided by Integrity, Compassion & Collaboration

Our Programs

Four focused programs that create lasting change in the lives of underserved communities.

Education

Education Initiatives

Tutoring, mentorship and learning resources that help children and young adults from underserved communities excel and pursue higher education.
