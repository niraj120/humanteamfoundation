Who We Are

Small acts of kindness, multiplied by people who care.

Human Team Foundation is a non-profit charitable trust driven by a simple belief — that a team of compassionate humans can solve the challenges no one should face alone.

  • 100% transparent, community-first approach
  • Every rupee tracked to real impact on the ground
  • Powered by passionate volunteers and donors

What We Do

Our Focus Areas

We work across the issues that matter most to the communities we serve.

Education

Schools, scholarships and learning centres so no child is left behind.

Healthcare

Free medical camps, medicines and awareness for underserved areas.

Nutrition

Daily meals and nutrition support for children and the elderly.

Women Empowerment

Skill training and livelihood support for financial independence.

Clean Water

Safe drinking water through wells, pumps and filtration.

Disaster Relief

Rapid food, shelter and essentials for families in crisis.
